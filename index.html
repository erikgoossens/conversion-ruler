Sheet metal gauge
Zoom Scale: 1000

Keyboard shortcuts & controls

Navigation

  • Left-click and drag anywhere to pan the ruler.
  • Trackpad scroll pans. Mouse wheel, pinch, or Ctrl+scroll zooms.

Conversion

  • The left half of the ruler is millimeters; the right half is inches. Move the pointer to the side you want to enter, then type a number. On the inches side, feel free to use spaces and forward-slashes for fractions (eg. 3 1/4). Hitting Enter converts it to a decimal, and provides the metric conversion.
  • Click a tick mark to load that value and convert it.
  • Click a conversion value to copy it to the clipboard.
  • Type in an expression like (1 3/4in) + 4*34mm + 2ft and hit enter to see the result in mm and in.
BackspaceDelete the last typed character.
EnterConfirm the typed value (for inch fractions)
CClear the typed value and any measured selection.

Measurement

  • Right-click and drag on empty ruler to add a measurement bar. The end snaps to the ticks on the side the pointer is on, and stops when it meets another bar. You can zoom while stretching.
  • Left- or right-click and drag a bar to move it. The half you grab is the end that snaps — upper half snaps the top, lower half snaps the bottom. A bar stops when it touches another and cannot pass through it.
  • Right-click a bar without dragging to remove just that bar. Left-click empty space to clear every measurement.
  • Click a measurement length or position to copy it to the clipboard.

Shortcuts

TThreads: Show/hide thread sizes.
GGauge: Show/hide sheet-metal gauge thicknesses. Choose a metal with the selector in the bottom-right.
DDecimal: Switch inch labels between fractions and decimals.

Navigation

  • Drag to pan
  • Pinch to zoom

Conversion

  • The left half of the ruler is millimeters; the right half is inches
  • Tap a tickmark on the ruler to load that value in inches and millimeters
  • Tap a conversion value in the upper left and right to copy it to clipboard
  • Tap+hold a conversion value in the upper left and right to type in a new one (you can do fractions like 1 5/8 on the inches side)

Measurement

  • Tap+hold on the ruler to select a value with the loupe
  • Tap “measure from here”
  • Tap+hold elsewhere on the ruler, and select “measure to here”
  • Drag the red bar to move it around. The half you grab is the end that snaps — upper half snaps the top, lower half snaps the bottom — to the increments on the side that your finger is on.
  • Tap empty space to clear the measurement

Shortcuts

  • Use the G button to overlay metal gauge thicknesses
  • Use the D button to toggle decimal view of inches
  • Use the T button to overlay imperial thread sizes
